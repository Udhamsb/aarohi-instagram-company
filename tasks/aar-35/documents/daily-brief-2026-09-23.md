## 2. Concept

**A desk-to-dinner Wednesday, told as one small ritual.** The working day ends; the one thing Aarohi changes is where she is sitting — from a work desk to a balcony table — and she carries the same cardigan, the same bag and the same evening light with her. The story is continuity, not transformation: one outfit doing a full day's work is exactly what the carousel is for.

Why this concept for this day: silhouette 5 is the calendar's desk-to-dinner garment, so the slides have to *show* a day that starts at a desk and ends at a table. The wardrobe is the story; nothing in frame needs to say so, because nothing in frame may say anything at all.

**Story spine (one sentence per slide, in order):** finishing the last thing on the desk → standing up and pulling the cardigan straight → a quick mirror-moment check → walking out through the doorway → the same outfit at the table, bag down, evening light.

## 4. Lighting and framing consistency (set-level, non-negotiable)

**One lighting family.** Warm late-afternoon daylight from **camera-left** in every slide — one large soft window as the key, gentle falloff to the right side of frame, no second light source.

- Light direction: single key from camera-left in **5/5** slides. Shadows fall to camera-right on every slide.
- Colour temperature: within **±200 K** of slide 1; warm, truthful, no cooling shift between slides (CAC-20).
- Shadow logic: soft, single-source, no harsh on-camera flash, no overhead downlight, no night interior.
- Finish: natural, realistic skin and knit texture; no hyper-gloss, no extreme blur, no voyeuristic depth-of-field.

**Consistent framing distance.** Waist-up medium framing on **5/5** slides, subject's eye line inside the middle third of the frame.

- Face-to-frame height fraction (top of head to chin as a fraction of frame height): within **±10%** of slide 1 across all slides (CAC-21).
- No close-up and no full-body mixed into a waist-up set.
- Camera height: eye level on all five. No low angle, no high angle, no tilt.
- Subject placement: centre-left with negative space at camera-right on all five (CAC-22). This also keeps the platform's own UI clear on a phone.
- Crop discipline: do not crop the forehead (the bindi must survive) or the hands where they carry the action.

**Why this is measured, not asserted:** "one coherent set" is the difference between a carousel and five unrelated pictures. A set that fails CAC-20/21/22 is regenerated as a set, not slide by slide — and a single failing slide puts the whole carousel back through the consolidated correction brief (AAR-25 §6).
