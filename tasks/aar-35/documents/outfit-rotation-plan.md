## How to read the locked strings

The **LOCKED OUTFIT STRING** in each day's row is the text that must appear **word-for-word** in every slide prompt for that date, and is the string the QC gate (CAC-6/CAC-7) matches against. Do not shorten, reorder or paraphrase it. The garment wording is taken verbatim from `AAROHI_WARDROBE_REFERENCE_v1.png` panels 1–8; the colourway is named by the Creative Director because the reference sheet fixes garment *types* and leaves colour open — naming it is what makes "one outfit" measurable across five slides (`CAC-8`).

Every day also carries the **item 8 accessory and jewellery lock**, which is worn on every post: silver jhumkas, small centred maroon bindi, hair long/open/dark, one small crossbody bag or tote, no body-emphasising accessories.

Sequence read as numbers: **5, 6, 1, 7, 6, 4, 6 — no value repeats in adjacent positions.** ✓

The three fitness days are **Thu 2026-09-24, Sun 2026-09-27 and Tue 2026-09-29** — silhouette 6 three times inside one Tue→Mon cycle. Their spacing is 3 days, 3 days, and 2 days (and the next cycle's Thursday fitness day falls 2 days after this Tuesday). **Every gap is two or more days, so no two fitness days are ever adjacent.** That spacing is precisely why this window is legal: with only one approved fitness silhouette, three fitness days cannot be placed without repeats, but across a Tue→Mon cycle the three repeats sit two or more days apart and the AAR-34 no-consecutive-repeat rule is never breached.

## Two things stated openly rather than buried

**1. The deviation from AAR-7 §4's weekday column — upheld by the ruling, unchanged in v1.1.** AAR-7 §4 lists silhouette 6 (fitness) as the Week 1 calendar's Mon · Thu · Sun wear — but in a Tue→Mon week, **Sun and Mon are consecutive days**, so wearing fitness on both would breach the AAR-34 rule that the outfit rotate day to day with no repeat on consecutive days. The two rules cannot both hold here. **Fitness stays on Thu 24 and Sun 27, and Mon 28 stays on silhouette 4** (relaxed top + straight trousers — an approved "general daytime" silhouette). Monday's *concept* may remain a home-and-reset lifestyle day; only the garment moves. AAR-34 `garment-authority-ruling` rev 1 point 4 confirms this reading: where two standards cannot both hold, the newer and more specific one governs, so the AAR-34 rotation rule beats the §4 weekday column. The v1.0 offer of a Mon 28 ↔ Tue 29 swap to keep the §4 row literally is therefore **closed by the ruling** — Tuesday now carries the fitness garment, and Monday's garment is untouched.

**2. Pillar mix — now delivered at fashion 2 / fitness 3 / lifestyle 2.** In v1.0 this plan landed at fashion 2 / fitness 2 / lifestyle 3 and named the legal route to a third fitness day: switch **Tue 2026-09-29 from silhouette 1 to silhouette 6**, which lifts fitness to 3 days against AAR-37's stated 40% / 35% / 25% mix without creating a consecutive repeat. That route is now taken, on AAR-37's pillar authority as confirmed by AAR-34 `garment-authority-ruling` rev 1 point 3. The window therefore reads **fashion 2 / fitness 3 / lifestyle 2**, and no further pillar change is required of this plan. Garment-level authority for every day remains this document.

## Rules that apply to every day in this rotation

- **One silhouette per day, held verbatim.** Every slide of that day's carousel carries the day's LOCKED OUTFIT STRING unchanged. No layer added or removed mid-carousel, no colourway shift.
- **Only the eight approved items** may appear; any garment not on the list is a **modesty breach and a REJECT — regenerate** (AAR-7 §4 usage rule 3).
- **Reject lists travel with the garment.** Item 5 rejects a camisole, strappy vest or any unapproved base; item 6 rejects a sports bra as outer layer, tight leggings, a tank or cropped top, and sheer panels; item 1 rejects sheer fabric, clingy fit, a low or deep neckline, a bare midriff and a slit above the knee; item 4 rejects crop length, bodycon knit, off-shoulder, and strap or camisole straps; item 7 rejects a slipping dupatta, a backless or low-neck kurta, a chiffon overlay and a tight fit; item 8 rejects tied or covered hair, heavy glam jewellery, body chains and any appearance-led styling.
- **Carousel spec per day:** 5 slides (4–6 permitted), all 4:5 portrait **1080 × 1350**, one lighting family, consistent framing distance, one story.
- **Frames stay text-free**, with **no on-image AI-generated disclosure mark** of any kind (AAR-33). Disclosure lives in the caption line plus the platform AI-content label.
- **This plan names garments only.** Concept, caption and hashtags are AAR-37's deliverable; the slide scenes and the prompt are AAR-36's and are briefed per day by the Creative Director. Spend stays behind the render-contract + live-estimate + explicit `confirm_cost` gate.
