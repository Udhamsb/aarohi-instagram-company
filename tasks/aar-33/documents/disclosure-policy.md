# Aarohi disclosure policy — no AI-generated text in the image (HARD RULE)

**Issued:** 2026-09-22 · **Issue:** AAR-33 · **Author:** Chief of Staff · **Authority:** direct board directive (user WQDCSqxCyGwdDVj9d5IDBZRvJFm99UYI)
**Status:** Binding, effective immediately, company-wide. Supersedes every earlier document that requires, describes, measures or QC-checks an on-image `AI-generated` disclosure.

## The rule

1. **No AI-generated disclosure text in the image or video frame — ever, from now on.** The `AI-generated` badge/overlay is retired from visual assets. It must not be:
   - requested in a generation prompt;
   - rendered by the image/video model;
   - burned in, composited, or added as a platform-native text layer inside the frame;
   - required, measured, or QC-gated on any delivered asset.
2. **The frame stays text-free.** This stacks with the existing clean-image rule (AAR-12/AAR-13/AAR-25 Z-1..Z-3): no captions, headings, labels, watermarks, logos, signage, pseudo-text or glyph-like marks anywhere in frame. The former exception for the disclosure badge is withdrawn, so the only remaining on-asset text exception is the AAR-13 §2 board-approved campaign-text path.
3. **Disclosure does not disappear — it moves entirely outside the frame.** Unchanged and still mandatory:
   - every caption carries the plain-language note that the visual is AI-generated and that caption/storytelling is human-directed (AAR-15 §5, AAR-24);
   - the platform-native AI-content label (Instagram `isAiGenerated: true` and the profile-level "AI-generated profile" label) stays enabled at publish.
   This keeps the account compliant with the Meta AI-disclosure rule and the Indian labelling/ASCI expectations recorded in AAR-24, while satisfying the directive that nothing AI-disclosure-related appears in the picture itself.
4. **Rejection standard updated.** An on-image `AI-generated` mark is now itself a defect: an asset carrying it fails QC and must be re-edited to a clean frame before handoff or publish. Missing caption disclosure, or a disabled platform label, is the disclosure breach instead.
5. **No retroactive rewrite of published work.** Assets already published or delivered keep their history as-is. Week 1 (25 published 9:16 assets carrying the badge) is not re-edited; AAR-32's reference-pack stills are internal-only and are not regenerated unless a new brief asks for them.

- Agent operating instructions (managed bundles): AI Visual Producer, Creative Director, Content Strategist & Copywriter, Community & Insights Manager.
- Runtime skills: AI Visual Producer `visual-asset-disclosure-qa`, Chief of Staff `social-content-creative-standards`.

## Verification of this policy

- Any Aarohi visual delivered after 2026-09-22 that contains `AI-generated`, `AI generated` or any glyph-like disclosure mark in the frame: **FAIL**.
- Any Aarohi post published with no caption disclosure and no platform AI label: **FAIL**.

Report exceptions to the Chief of Staff on this issue.
