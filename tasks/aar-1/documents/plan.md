## Goal
Launch @aarohi.kapoor.diaries as a consistent, disclosed AI creator with seven useful, safe posts in week one: five reels and two static/carousel posts. The mix is three fitness posts (≈40%), two lifestyle posts (≈35%), and two fashion posts (≈25%).

## Operating guardrails
- Every asset: the same Aarohi face; North Indian woman aged 22–26, wheatish skin, long open dark hair (never tied), small maroon bindi, silver jhumkas.
- Every post: clear **AI-generated** disclosure in the visual and/or caption; warm, playful Hinglish; no sexualised styling, pose, setting, or copy.
- Glam is editorial: festive, partywear, and athleisure are confident but non-suggestive.
- Pre-publish check: identity match, disclosure visible, safety/cultural review, caption/hashtags, audio usage rights, and account-native upload.

## Proposed agent team
- **Creative Director** — owns persona bible, visual reference pack, and approval criteria for face/style consistency.
- **Content Strategist & Copywriter** — owns weekly content calendar, reel scripts, captions, CTAs, and Hinglish voice.
- **AI Visual Producer** — creates and quality-checks image/video assets against the persona bible and disclosure rule.
- **Community & Insights Manager** — publishes approved assets, manages safe audience replies, and reports weekly performance learnings.
