## Standard

Image-overlay text is retired. Every post ships with a clean, text-free
image and the full story lives in the caption. This template is the
default for all Aarohi copy going forward.

## Caption structure (required, in order)

1. **Hook** — one specific, human line tied to the actual visual. No
   generic openers ("Feeling blessed", "New day, new me").
2. **Context** — 1-2 short lines on what is happening and why it matters.
3. **Value or feeling** — one line: a useful takeaway or an honest
   reflection. Not a slogan.
4. **CTA** — one relevant question or action. Never more than one, never
   clickbait ("You won't believe...").
5. **Disclosure (when applicable)** — plain-language note that the visual
   is AI-generated when the post could otherwise read as an unstaged
   photo; caption/storytelling is human-directed.
6. **Hashtags** — 3-6 hashtags, specific to the post's subject/setting,
   no generic spam blocks (no #love #instagood #photooftheday stacking).

## Writing rules

- Natural, concise, conversational. Write like a person describing their
  own moment, not ad copy.
- Every line must be traceable to something actually in the image/video
  (setting, activity, mood, light, wardrobe). No invented events, no fake
  quotes, no claims the visual doesn't support.
- No generic AI phrasing: avoid "Embrace the journey", "Living my best
  life", "Elevate your everyday", "Let's dive in", em-dash-heavy filler,
  or listicle-style adjectives stacked before nouns.
- No clickbait, no manufactured urgency, no irrelevant text.
- One CTA only, phrased as a genuine question or simple action, not a
  demand.
- Disclosure is short and factual, not defensive or apologetic.

## Pre-publish check (unchanged from AAR-12, applies to every caption)

- Caption is concise, natural, and specific to the visual — nothing
  generic or reusable across unrelated images.
- Exactly one CTA, phrased as a question or simple action.
- Disclosure included whenever the post could read as an unstaged photo
  or involves a paid/gifted relationship.
- 3-6 hashtags, all relevant to the subject or setting shown.
- No text baked into the image; the caption carries the full story.
