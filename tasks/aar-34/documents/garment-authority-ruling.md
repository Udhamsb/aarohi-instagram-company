# AAR-34 ruling — single garment authority for the daily carousel

**Issued:** 2026-09-22 · **Issue:** AAR-34 · **Author:** Chief of Staff · **Authority:** AAR-34 `daily-format-standard` (the binding rule); arbitration of a cross-document conflict surfaced by the Creative Director on AAR-35.
**Applies to:** every Aarohi daily carousel from 2026-09-23 onward.

Neither document contradicted the other's *format* rules: both carry the 5-slide 4:5 1080×1350 spec, the zero-text gate, the no-on-image-disclosure rule, and the one-outfit-per-day lock. The disagreement is confined to which silhouette sits on which day.

## The ruling

**1. AAR-35 `outfit-rotation-plan` is the single garment authority for the daily carousel.**

Where the two documents differ on garments, AAR-35 wins. It carries the verbatim per-day LOCKED OUTFIT STRING, the named colourway, the per-garment reject lists, and the consecutive-day proof; the calendar names silhouettes by number only. That is the deciding reason — an authority that cannot express the garment precisely cannot arbitrate it. This also matches the standing split of ownership: creative direction owns the wardrobe and approval criteria, copy owns the calendar and pillar weighting.

**2. Fri 2026-09-25 = silhouette #1, Mon 2026-09-28 = silhouette #4.** A straight transposition resolved in AAR-35's favour. Both silhouettes are approved for these days by AAR-7 §4, so no wardrobe rule is engaged and no new garment string is required.

**3. Tue 2026-09-29 = silhouette #6 (fitness). The Content Strategist's correction stands.**

This is the one place where the calendar wins, and it is deliberate rather than a tie-break:

- AAR-7 §4 maps Tuesday to the lifestyle row, which is why AAR-35 left it at #1.
- AAR-35's own plan states that the legal way to reach the fitness pillar target is to switch Tue #1 → #6, and that **"the Content Strategist owns the pillar weighting."** The Creative Director declined the switch only to defer to that ownership.
- The calendar's switch to #6 lifts the window to **fitness 3 / lifestyle 2 / fashion 2**, which is the closest whole-day fit to the standing 40/35/25 mix.
- With one outfit per day and no consecutive repeat, and only one approved fitness silhouette, this is the only arrangement that reaches 3 fitness days.

So the ruling is not "one document beats the other everywhere". It is: **AAR-35 governs the garment; AAR-37 governs the pillar, and the pillar choice legitimately moves one day's garment.** That is exactly the division the Creative Director named.

**4. AAR-35's Monday deviation from AAR-7 §4 stands.** AAR-7 maps fitness to Mon · Thu · Sun, but under a Tue→Mon cycle Sun and Mon are consecutive days, and holding fitness on both would breach the one-outfit-per-day no-repeat rule. Where two standards cannot both hold, the newer and more specific one governs: the AAR-34 `daily-format-standard` rotation rule beats the AAR-7 §4 weekday column. Monday's concept may remain a home-and-reset lifestyle day; only the garment moves.

**5. Consequence for the window 2026-09-23 → 2026-09-29:** pillar mix **fashion 2 / fitness 3 / lifestyle 2**; silhouette sequence **5, 6, 1, 7, 6, 4, 6**. No silhouette repeats on consecutive days: read as numbers, no adjacent pair is equal, and the two repeats of #6 (Thu, Sun, Tue) sit at least two days apart.

**6. The 2026-09-23 first post is untouched.** All documents already agreed on silhouette #5, so the held AAR-36 render contract, the approved 10-credit charge, and the funding gate are unaffected by this ruling.

## What this does not change

- AAR-34 `daily-format-standard` remains the binding rule; this ruling only resolves which document speaks for the garment inside it.
- No on-image AI-generated disclosure (AAR-33). Frames stay text-free.
- Caption disclosure line plus the platform AI-content label stay mandatory.
- No billable render without a recorded render contract, a live estimate, and `confirm_cost` behind explicit human approval.
- Nothing already published is re-edited. The Tuesday 2026-09-22 carousel stands.
