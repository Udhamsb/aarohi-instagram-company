# Aarohi daily publishing format — image carousel only, one outfit per day (HARD RULE)

**Issued:** 2026-09-22 · **Issue:** AAR-34 · **Author:** Chief of Staff · **Authority:** direct board directive (user WQDCSqxCyGwdDVj9d5IDBZRvJFm99UYI)
**Status:** Binding, effective immediately, company-wide. Supersedes every earlier Aarohi plan, calendar, brief, prompt package or production standard that specifies a reel, video, static-photo or mixed-format daily slot.

## The rule

1. **One post per day, and it is an image carousel.** Every daily slot is a carousel of still images. Reels, video posts and single-static posts are **discontinued as daily formats**.
2. **One outfit per day — worn consistently across every slide of that day's carousel.** The same garments, colours, layering and accessories appear on all slides of the post. No outfit change mid-carousel, no per-slide wardrobe variation.
3. **The outfit must come from the approved wardrobe list** (AAR-7 §4 / `AAROHI_WARDROBE_REFERENCE_v1.png`). The outfit **rotates day to day**: each day takes a different approved silhouette so consecutive posts do not repeat. Any garment outside that list stays a modesty breach and a reject.
4. **Carousel spec.** 4–6 slides (default 5), all slides **4:5 portrait, 1080×1350** — the platform's carousel aspect ceiling and the ratio AAR-24 already recommended for carousels. One coherent set: same person, one lighting family, consistent framing distance, one story across the slides. The exact spec must be stated in the brief and **measured on delivery**, never assumed.
5. **Everything else stands unchanged:** text-free frames (AAR-12 / AAR-13 / AAR-25 zero-text), **no on-image AI-generated disclosure** (AAR-33 `disclosure-policy`), caption-first copy with the mandatory caption disclosure line plus the platform AI-content label (`isAiGenerated` true and the profile-level "AI-generated profile" label), the locked identity and modesty rules, and the billable-render gate (AAR-27 / AAR-29 render contract + live estimate + `confirm_cost` behind explicit human approval).
6. **What this retires as a deliverable:** reel scripts, video prompts, silent-master and audio-stream requirements, and the reel-format QC gates. Those documents stay on the record as history and as the QC basis for any video asset the board explicitly re-authorises later. They are not a default deliverable and must not be produced speculatively.
7. **No retroactive rewrite.** The published Tuesday 2026-09-22 5-slide carousel (Zernio `6ab21c1013e2cd514b3d63d7`) stands as published. Already-scheduled slots whose format contradicts this rule **must not publish in the old format**: the publishing owner replaces the slot's media with the approved daily-format carousel before its publish time, or cancels the slot, and reports each slot's final state.

## One reading decision, stated openly

"Same outfit" is read here as **one outfit per day, consistent across that day's slides, rotating day to day** — that is what makes a carousel read as one coherent moment, and it is the reading that produces a workable daily calendar. If the board meant the literally identical outfit on **every** day, that single line changes and nothing else in this standard moves.

## Cadence and slot

One carousel per day, published at the account's consistent slot **20:00 IST (14:30 UTC)** — the slot Week 1 settled on. Weekday alignment of the concept is kept.

## Verification of this standard

- Any Aarohi daily post delivered after 2026-09-22 that is a reel, a video or a single static image: **FAIL**.
- Any daily carousel whose slides do not all show the same single outfit, or whose slides are not 4:5 1080×1350: **FAIL**.
- Any carousel using a garment outside the AAR-7 §4 approved list: **FAIL** (modesty breach).
- Any frame carrying on-image text or an AI-generated mark: **FAIL** (AAR-33).

Report exceptions to the Chief of Staff on this issue.
