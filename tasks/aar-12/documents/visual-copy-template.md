## Non-negotiable image rule

- Keep the image text-free by default.
- Never place AI-style headings, fake quotes, labels, watermarks, captions, CTAs, hashtags, or decorative typography on the image.
- If text is explicitly approved for a campaign, use only the exact approved copy, keep it minimal and legible, and produce a clean no-text variant as well.
- Put context, storytelling, calls to action, disclosure, and hashtags in the caption or reel-native text layer, not baked into the visual.

## Image-generation brief

Subject: [Aarohi / approved subject]

Do not include: any text, letters, logos, watermarks, UI elements, posters, signs with legible words, distorted hands, duplicated objects, plastic-looking skin, oversharpening, or irrelevant decorative elements.

## Pre-publish check

- Image has no unintended text or AI artifacts.
- Image supports the caption; it does not repeat or replace it.
- Caption is concise, natural, and relevant to the visual.
- CTA is specific and optional, not clickbait.
- Model selection and output resolution are documented in the asset record.
